# Fee policy (Vidya Coaching Centre)

Agreed with the owner, Mrs. Kulkarni, April 2026. The app should follow this.

## Installments

- Each batch has a monthly fee and a number of installments.
- The first installment is due on the batch start date. Each later one is due on the same day of the following months.
- If a month doesn't have that day (for example the 31st), the installment is due on the **last day of that month**.

## Late fee

- ₹50 for **each completed week** an installment stays unpaid after its due date.
- A partial week is not charged. 6 days late is ₹0; 7 to 13 days late is ₹50; 14 days late is ₹100.

## Paused batches

- A batch can be paused (for example during board exams). While a batch is paused, its installments are not counted as overdue and no late fee builds up.
- Parents can still pay during a pause, and those payments must show up everywhere as normal.

## Payments

- Payments are taken at the front desk (cash, UPI at the counter, cheque) or online through the payment gateway.
- Fees include GST at 18%. The receipt shows the GST portion.
- **The cash book counts every payment by its date and time in India (IST).** The monthly collection report must match the cash book.
